Kronoloop

Kronoloop makes short animated stories about history and how things work, published on the Kronoloop Facebook page (Tagalog) and the Kronoloop YouTube channel (English).

Kronoloop Factory

Kronoloop Factory is a private tool that the channel's owner runs on his own computer to upload and schedule his own videos to the Kronoloop Facebook page and the Kronoloop YouTube channel. It is not offered to the public.
