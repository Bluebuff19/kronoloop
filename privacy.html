Privacy policy

Kronoloop Factory · last updated November 21, 2026

What the app is

Kronoloop Factory is a private tool used only by the owner of the Kronoloop Facebook page and the Kronoloop YouTube channel, on his own computer, to upload and schedule his own videos. It is not offered to anyone else.

What it accesses

  • YouTube (Google): permission to upload videos to, and manage videos on, the owner's own channel, and to read the channel's name. Used only to publish and schedule Kronoloop videos.
  • Facebook (Meta): permission to publish videos and reels to the owner's own Kronoloop page and to read the page's name and its videos. Used only to publish and schedule Kronoloop videos.

What it stores

The access keys (tokens) are stored only on the owner's computer. The app does not collect, store, sell or share any personal information of viewers or of any other person, and it does not send data anywhere except to Google (YouTube) and Meta (Facebook) to publish the owner's own videos.

Google user data

Kronoloop Factory's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access and data

Access can be removed at any time in your Google account (myaccount.google.com/permissions) or in Facebook settings under Business integrations. Removing it stops the app from reaching the account; the tokens on the owner's computer are then deleted.
